About KARVO

One platform for getting your project done

KARVO brings fabrication, interiors, industrial work, renovation, maintenance and skilled professionals into a single customer-friendly service experience. KARVO is operated by NEOCRAFT LLP.

Customers can explore design inspiration, request quotations, discuss custom dimensions and enquire about site visits and professional teams. Prices displayed on the platform are indicative unless expressly confirmed in a signed quotation.

Actual work is subject to site conditions, measurements, scope approvals, availability and a separate quotation or work order. Images shown on the demo platform are AI-generated concepts, not photographs of completed client projects or specific employees.
